Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Noxe collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (for sign-in and cloud sync).
  • Content you create: profile details, ratings, notes, comments, and messages.
  • Photos you choose to add — only the specific images you pick.
  • Connected accounts (optional): if you link Steam, we import your public game hours; if you link Spotify, we import your top tracks and artists. Both are used to build your taste profile, and you can disconnect either at any time.
  • Push notification token: if you turn on notifications, your device's push token, so we can tell you about likes, comments, follows, and messages. Turning notifications off stops this.

How we use it

To run your account, sync your data across your devices, and power taste-matching and recommendations. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it's stored

With our backend provider (Supabase) and cached locally on your device.

Your choices

Edit or remove your data in the app, disconnect linked accounts, or permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete account.

Children

Noxe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Privacy questions: help@noxeteam.com

Community Guidelines

Not allowed
